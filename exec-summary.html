The Situation

Bradley Company is the Midwest's largest independently owned commercial real estate firm — 63 brokers across Michigan and Indiana, generating $540M+ in annual revenue. But today those brokers work in 3+ separate tools (Excel, Pipe Drive, disconnected systems), Brianna's team spends 6–8 hours per week manually stitching two separate AR systems, and commission tracking, invoice gen, and AR aging are managed in a redundant system alongside Yardi — significant unnecessary tech spend with no integration. Every week.

Salesforce connects to Yardi Voyager, ClickUp, and Outlook through open APIs — and enables commission tracking, invoice generation, and AR aging natively, solving the dual-AR-system problem and reducing redundant tech spend. Fortimize's proven CRE brokerage accelerator delivers 60–70% of tenant rep and investment sales workflows ready on day one, and bi-weekly sprints with broker feedback built in mean brokers shape the tool before it ships. Agentforce — the mobile-first AI assistant — is the easy button brokers have been asking for, and the reason this moment is different from every prior evaluation.
