Layer 1 · Data Sources
DLAS + Yardi Voyager
DLAS — deal pipeline, property listings, comps, and broker payroll all flow into Salesforce via open API. No duplication, no migration.
Yardi Voyager — property management accounting and financial reporting surfaces in dashboards without manual extraction or re-keying.
Zero replacement — both systems stay exactly as-is. Salesforce federates the data at the layer above, not in place of them.
"Bradley's $540M revenue runs through DLAS and Yardi. Neither goes away — Salesforce makes that data useful across the whole business."
Layer 2 · Federation
Salesforce Data Cloud
Zero-Copy Federation — DLAS and Yardi data appears in Salesforce without ETL pipelines, warehousing, or duplication. One source of truth.
Unified Broker Profiles — contact history, deal pipeline, communication logs, and property data merged into a single governed view per broker.
Real-Time Streaming — data refreshes live as brokers log voice notes in the field. Agentforce and Tableau both run from the same real-time source.
"Data Cloud is what makes Agentforce actually smart — it gives the AI a complete, real-time picture of every broker, deal, and property without IT building a data warehouse."
Layer 3 · Security
Einstein Trust Layer
Zero Data Retention — Bradley's deal data, client contacts, and financials are never stored by the AI model. Every prompt is processed and immediately discarded.
PII Masking — personally identifiable information is automatically detected and masked before it reaches the AI layer. No configuration required.
Full Audit Trail — every AI action logged with timestamp, user, and reasoning. Complete compliance record with zero additional setup.
"Same security architecture as CrowdStrike, RBC, and Toyota — enterprise-grade, no compromise. Bradley's client data stays Bradley's."
Layer 4 · AI · Mobile-First
Agentforce (AWS Bedrock)
Lead Scoring — "Show me my top leads near Grand Rapids today." Agentforce returns ranked results with AI scores in 10 seconds flat.
Voice Logging — one voice memo creates the activity record, opportunity update, and follow-up task automatically. Zero manual entry.
Model-Agnostic via AWS Bedrock — no vendor lock-in, no unpredictable token costs. Best model for each task, always.
"The LinkedIn congratulation email drafted live on Aug 31 came from Agentforce reading contact history through this exact stack — no prompt engineering, no setup."
Layer 5 · Applications
Sales Cloud + Tableau
Broker Command Center — AI-ranked leads, logged activities, deal stages, and tasks in one mobile-first view. No app switching, no spreadsheets.
Live Market Dashboards — Tableau dashboards per market and broker, updated in real time as activity is logged from the field. No Monday morning catch-up.
Executive Mobile View — Chad sees the full company picture from a phone app. No prep, no waiting, no stitched-together spreadsheets.
"John gets real-time pipeline visibility without a single status email from his team. Chad sees the full picture from his phone on Monday morning."
